**Color System: Dark Neo-Brutalist**

* **Base Background:** Pitch Black (`#000000` or `#0A0A0A`) or Deep Charcoal (`#151515`) to create a boundless, high-contrast canvas.
* **Structural Elements:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for all wireframing, borders, dividers, and primary text.
* **Action Accents (Choose 1 or 2 maximum):**
* *Industrial Orange* (`#FF5500` or `#FF7300`): Use for primary CTAs, destructive actions, or raw energy highlights.
* *Acid Green* (`#C4F000` or `#CCFF00`): Use for success states, syntax highlights, or active terminal cursors. (Passes WCAG AAA contrast standards against true black).



**Typography: Aggressive & Structural**

* **Headings:** Oversized, ultra-heavy weight (Black or ExtraBold), uppercase sans-serif. These should dominate the visual space and act as structural anchors rather than just text.
* **Code & Body:** Crisp, legible monospace or geometric sans-serif to contrast the heavy headers. Keep weights regular or medium to ensure readability during long coding sessions.
* **Hierarchy:** Rely strictly on massive scale contrast between headings and body text rather than varying shades of gray.

**UI Components & Geometry**

* **Borders & Shapes:** Strict geometric enforcement. Use thick (2px–4px) Pure White borders for all containers, input fields, and buttons. Zero border-radius (sharp 90-degree corners).
* **Motifs & Textures:** Incorporate raw structural elements like 1px dot-matrix grids in empty spaces, or hazard-style diagonal stripes for loading bars and active selection states.
* **Depth & Shadows:** Absolutely no gradients, blurs, or soft drop-shadows. If depth is required, use solid, offset hard shadows (100% opacity, sharp edges) in an accent color or pure white.

**Iconography & Imagery**

* **Style:** Flat, comic-book-style vector line art.
* **Execution:** Maintain a uniform, thick stroke weight for all icons.
* **Fills:** Keep base elements monochrome (black and white) with stark, flat fills of the chosen accent colors (Orange or Acid Green) to draw attention. No subtle shading or dimension.
